Set up Gated

  1. Open Gated and allow Screen Time access.
  2. Pick the apps and websites you want to lock, then tap Done.
  3. Allow notifications.

Unlock an app

  1. Open a locked app and tap Unlock on the block screen.
  2. Tap the notification that appears. Gated opens.
  3. Hold a number from 1 to 5. Each minute takes 10 seconds of holding.
  4. Take one slow breath per minute. The apps unlock for that many minutes.

Unlock does nothing

Gated needs notifications to bring you back from the block screen. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, tap Gated, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Remove every lock

Delete Gated from your iPhone. Every lock lifts right away.
