Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Gated collects no data. Everything it stores stays on your iPhone.

What Gated stores on your iPhone

  • The apps and websites you choose to lock. Apple's Screen Time gives Gated these as private tokens. Gated does not store their names and cannot see what you do inside them. The block screen shows an app's name only while it is on screen.
  • Your settings: lock times, your daily limit, Lock now, Strict mode, and when you last used the emergency pass.
  • When you unlocked and for how many minutes, in total and for each app or website you unlocked, so Gated can show your minutes for today and this week. Gated keeps five weeks of this.

What Gated never does

  • Gated has no accounts and no servers. It sends nothing off your iPhone.
  • Gated has no ads, analytics, or tracking, and shares nothing with anyone.
  • Gated never sees your messages, photos, browsing, or anything inside other apps.

Most used today

The Gated apps screen can show the apps you used most today. Apple's Screen Time draws that row on your iPhone. Gated never receives the list, and it never leaves your iPhone.

Screen Time and notifications

Gated asks for Screen Time access so it can lock the apps you choose. On iOS versions before 26.5 it also asks for notifications, so the button on the block screen can bring you back to Gated. Both stay on your iPhone, and you can turn either off in the iPhone Settings app.

Deleting your data

Delete Gated from your iPhone. That removes everything it stored and lifts every lock.

Children

Gated is a self-control tool for the person who owns the iPhone. It does not monitor anyone else and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
